Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This site is run by Chris McCann. Here is what happens with your information when you visit chrismccann.co.

What I collect

If you subscribe to Field Notes or take the Field Assessment, I collect your email address. That email goes to Beehiiv, which handles the newsletter. I use it to send you what I said I would send you. Nothing else.

If you take the Field Assessment, your results (score band, dimension scores) are attached to your subscription so I can send a relevant follow-up. No answers to individual questions are stored.

Analytics

This site uses Plausible Analytics, which is privacy-focused and does not use cookies. No personal data is collected. No tracking across sites. Plausible collects aggregate data only: page views, referral sources, country, device type. None of it is tied to an individual.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies.

Third parties

Beehiiv (newsletter delivery), Netlify (hosting), Plausible (analytics), Google Fonts (typography). Each has its own privacy policy. I chose them because they are straightforward about what they do with data.

Your data

If you want to unsubscribe, every email has a link at the bottom. If you want your email removed entirely, write me at chris@chrismccann.co and I will take care of it.

Contact

Questions about any of this: chris@chrismccann.co
